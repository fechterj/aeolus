AEOLUS

  • Drag to move · scroll / pinch or + / − to zoom · double-click zooms in
  • Click anywhere for that point's wind, gusts, temperature and 24-hour outlook
  • ▶ / space plays time forward — watch weather systems travel
  • Slider scrubs up to 3 days of forecast · NOW returns to the present
  • Layers — toggle wind streamlines, the temperature field and live rain
  • Silk — long-exposure mode: the wind paints itself into ribbons of light
  • Cinema — everything disappears and the atmosphere performs · Capture saves it as a poster
  • Globe (G) — the same wind, temperature and rain wrapping a turning night Earth, with the lights of more than 500 cities
  • Gallery — every sky you capture is kept on this device; cinema collects them as it plays
  • Search flies to any place on Earth
  • Share — a link that opens this exact view: place, zoom, layers, time and the point you opened. The address bar keeps up as you move
  • Keyboard — arrow keys move the view (Shift for bigger steps) · Enter shows the weather at the centre
  • Every streamline is real air: thousands of particles riding the actual forecast wind field, colored by speed
Wind data: Open-Meteo blended forecast models (ICON · GFS · ECMWF), updated hourly, free & open. Basemap: Esri dark gray canvas (Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and the GIS user community). No accounts, no keys, no tracking — just the atmosphere.
